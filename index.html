The Menagerie Wellness Index

A score that means something.

Built on the Five Domains model — the animal welfare framework used in zoos and veterinary science — the Wellness Index reads your care logs and sensor data to show how each animal is actually doing. Not a streak counter. A welfare instrument.

Wellness Index · Waffles

i.

Nutrition

Feeding frequency against species norms, feeding response, weight trend. A tarantula fasting three weeks is normal. A tegu isn't.

ii.

Environment

Temperature gradients, humidity, UVB age, photoperiod — with continuous sensor data weighted above manual logs.

iii.

Health

Shed and molt quality, waste consistency, medication compliance, body condition. A clean shed says a lot.

iv.

Behavior

Activity, enrichment, sightings, seasonal patterns — weighted for what's natural per species. Hiding isn't penalized.

Built for the way keepers actually keep

Tracks things you never thought to track.

◉ Sensors that log for you

Govee and SwitchBot integration streams temperature and humidity straight into each enclosure's record. No more clipboard checks.

☾ Sky stamps on every log

Moon phase, barometric pressure, and geomagnetic activity recorded automatically. Patterns you'd never catch by hand.

❋ Living terrariums

Bioactive enclosures tracked as ecosystems — establishment phases, seasons, provisions, plants, and the microfauna underneath it all.

⌂ The whole household

Share care with family and petsitters. Everyone logs to the same record, every animal stays covered.

✳ Every species

Reptiles, arachnids, myriapods, amphibians, mammals — species-aware care logic for each. One app for the entire collection.

◬ Citizen science

Opt in to contribute anonymized care data to the first longitudinal welfare dataset for captive exotic species.
